## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Spoken Script & Screen Action Guide

---

### 📍 STEP 1: Problem & Vision (0:00 – 0:20 | 20 Secs)

* **🖥️ Screen Action:** Open **Executive Dashboard** (`http://localhost:5173/`).
* **🗣️ Spoken Script:**
  > *"Hello everyone! Today I’m presenting **COMMIT** — an intelligent B2B Receivables & Payment Recovery OS.  
  > In B2B commerce, accounting software tracks unpaid invoices, but completely ignores informal payment promises made over email or WhatsApp (*'I’ll pay ₹30k tomorrow and ₹20k on Friday'*).  
  > COMMIT bridges this gap by converting raw customer promises into tracked, policy-enforced financial commitments."*

---

### 📍 STEP 2: Executive Dashboard & Live Metrics (0:20 – 0:45 | 25 Secs)

* **🖥️ Screen Action:** Point mouse to **Total Overdue (₹50,000)**, **Verified Paid**, and scroll to **Aging Risk Buckets**.
* **🗣️ Spoken Script:**
  > *"Here on the **Executive Dashboard**, finance teams get instant, real-time metrics:  
  > Total Overdue Balances, Active Promised Amounts, Verified Bank Receipts, and Recovery Efficiency.  
  > The system automatically segments accounts into **Low, Medium, High, and Critical Risk** buckets based on payment history and broken promises."*

---

### 📍 STEP 3: B2B Invoices & Razorpay Payment Gateway (0:45 – 1:10 | 25 Secs)

* **🖥️ Screen Action:** Click **Invoices** tab → Click **INV-1001** → Click **Pay via Razorpay**.
* **🗣️ Spoken Script:**
  > *"Under the **Invoices** tab, we manage our complete B2B receivables ledger.  
  > Clicking on invoice **INV-1001** opens its line items and current balance.  
  > When I click **'Pay via Razorpay'**, COMMIT instantly generates an online Razorpay payment order link tied directly to the outstanding balance for instant digital collection."*

---

### 📍 STEP 4: 🌟 HERO STEP — AI Commitment Extraction (1:10 – 1:45 | 35 Secs)

* **🖥️ Screen Action:** Click **Customer Inbox** → Click Email Thread → Paste text: *"I'll pay ₹30,000 tomorrow and ₹20,000 by Friday."* → Click **Extract Commitment**.
* **🗣️ Spoken Script:**
  > *(Speak with enthusiasm & focus!)*  
  > *"Now for our **Hero Feature — AI Commitment Extraction.**  
  > When a customer emails: **'I'll pay ₹30,000 tomorrow and remaining ₹20,000 by Friday'**,  
  > COMMIT's NLP extraction engine instantly parses the text into two structured micro-commitments:  
  > 1️⃣ **₹30,000 due tomorrow**  
  > 2️⃣ **₹20,000 due Friday**  
  > Unstructured text is now converted into structured, enforceable financial records!"*

---

### 📍 STEP 5: Commitment Tracker & State Machine Engine (1:45 – 2:10 | 25 Secs)

* **🖥️ Screen Action:** Click **Commitments** tab → Point to `ACTIVE`, `FULFILLED`, `BROKEN` badges.
* **🗣️ Spoken Script:**
  > *"On the **Commitment Tracker**, these extracted promises enter our **Strict State Machine Engine**.  
  > Promises transition through verified states: **ACTIVE**, **FULFILLED**, **PARTIAL**, **BROKEN**, or **EXPIRED**.  
  > If a payment arrives at the bank, the state turns FULFILLED; if the date passes without payment, it automatically turns BROKEN."*

---

### 📍 STEP 6: Decision Engine & Automated Batch Evaluation (2:10 – 2:30 | 20 Secs)

* **🖥️ Screen Action:** Click **Decision Engine** → Click **Batch Evaluation** → Click **Run Batch Scan**.
* **🗣️ Spoken Script:**
  > *"When a promise breaks, our **Recovery Decision Engine** evaluates grace periods, confidence scores, and risk bands.  
  > Clicking **'Run Batch Evaluation'** triggers automated scanning across all aging accounts, scheduling reminder actions and policy-enforced escalations without manual effort."*

---

### 📍 STEP 7: Exception Resolution Center (2:30 – 2:45 | 15 Secs)

* **🖥️ Screen Action:** Click **Exceptions** tab → Click **Resolve Case**.
* **🗣️ Spoken Script:**
  > *"For ambiguous promises or payment discrepancies, the **Exception Center** provides a human-in-the-loop review queue where AR managers can inspect evidence and resolve cases with full compliance."*

---

### 📍 STEP 8: Audit Trail & Webhook Auto-Reconciliation (2:45 – 3:00 | 15 Secs)

* **🖥️ Screen Action:** Click **Live Sandbox** → Fast-forward date → Click **Simulate Razorpay Webhook**.
* **🗣️ Spoken Script:**
  > *"Finally, in our **Live Sandbox**, we simulate a live Razorpay `payment.captured` webhook.  
  > COMMIT verifies the **HMAC SHA256 signature**, checks idempotency, auto-reconciles the payment, marks the commitment FULFILLED, and logs everything into an immutable **Audit Trail**.  
  > Thank you for reviewing COMMIT!"*
