# 🏆 3-Minute Demo Presentation Script & Hero Feature Guide

---

## ⭐️ THE #1 BEST FEATURE TO HIGHLIGHT & FOCUS ON

If you have to showcase **ONE killer feature** to wow judges, evaluators, or investors:

### 🥇 **Hero Feature: AI-Powered Payment Commitment Extraction & State Engine**
> **Why it's unique & powerful:**  
> In B2B commerce, 90% of receivables delays happen because customer payment promises made over email or WhatsApp ("*We will pay ₹30k tomorrow and ₹20k on Friday*") are informal, unrecorded, and forgotten.  
> 
> **COMMIT solves this by:**
> 1. Automatically parsing raw text into structured micro-commitments with target dates & confidence scores.
> 2. Enforcing a **Strict Lifecycle State Engine** (`ACTIVE` → `FULFILLED` / `BROKEN` / `PARTIAL`).
> 3. Fast-forwarding time to show how **broken promises automatically trigger policy-driven recovery actions**.
